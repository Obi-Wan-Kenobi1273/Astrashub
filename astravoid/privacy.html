AstraVoid Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AstraVoid processes information when it is installed on or used in a Discord server.

1. About AstraVoid

AstraVoid is a Discord moderation bot designed to provide moderation and server-management features to Discord communities.

AstraVoid operates through Discord's platform and therefore interacts with information made available to it through Discord and the permissions granted to the bot by a server.

2. Information AstraVoid May Process

Depending on the commands and features being used, AstraVoid may process the following types of information:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server (guild) IDs
  • Discord channel IDs
  • Discord role IDs
  • Usernames, display names, and mentions
  • Moderation-related information
  • Warning reasons entered by moderators
  • Information required to perform moderation actions
  • Command interaction information
  • Bot configuration and logging information

AstraVoid only requests and processes information that is reasonably necessary for the bot's features to function.

3. Moderation Data

AstraVoid may process moderation information when a server moderator uses moderation commands. This may include the Discord ID of the moderator, the Discord ID of the affected member, the action performed, and information such as a warning reason.

Moderation information may be sent to a server's designated moderation logging channel when the server has configured one.

4. Message Content

AstraVoid does not require access to the contents of every message in a Discord server simply because it is installed there.

If a future AstraVoid feature requires access to message content, that feature will only use such information for its stated functionality and this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

5. How Information Is Used

Information processed by AstraVoid may be used to:

  • Execute moderation commands requested by authorized users
  • Send moderation notifications
  • Record moderation actions in configured server logging channels
  • Identify Discord users, servers, channels, and roles required by commands
  • Maintain and operate AstraVoid's features
  • Prevent misuse of the bot
  • Troubleshoot technical problems

6. Data Storage

AstraVoid may store limited configuration or moderation-related information when required for a feature to operate.

Data is not intentionally collected for advertising, marketing, or the sale of personal information.

We aim to limit stored information to what is reasonably necessary for AstraVoid to provide its functionality.

7. Data Sharing

AstraVoid does not sell personal information.

Information processed by AstraVoid may be transmitted through Discord's services because AstraVoid operates as a Discord application.

Information may also be processed by infrastructure or hosting providers required to operate the bot.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant AstraVoid feature, server configuration, moderation functionality, or technical operation.

Server administrators may request removal of AstraVoid-specific stored information associated with their server where applicable.

9. Removing AstraVoid

A server administrator can remove AstraVoid from their Discord server at any time.

Removing the bot prevents it from continuing to operate in that server, although information that was already recorded in Discord channels or otherwise retained for legitimate operational purposes may not automatically disappear.

10. Children's Privacy

AstraVoid is a Discord application and is subject to Discord's own age requirements and policies. AstraVoid does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children independently of Discord.

11. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by AstraVoid. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AstraVoid's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be made available on this page.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or AstraVoid's handling of information, contact the AstraVoid support team at astravoid.support@gmail.com .
