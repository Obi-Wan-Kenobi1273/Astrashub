AstraBot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Introduction

AstraBot is a Discord bot developed and operated by Astra. This Privacy Policy explains what information AstraBot may collect, how it is used, and how it is stored.

2. Information AstraBot May Store

Depending on the features used, AstraBot may store Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames or display names, virtual currency balances, inventory information, achievements, titles, game statistics, moderation records, and other information required for its features.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide and operate AstraBot's features, including economy systems, profiles, games, achievements, titles, moderation tools, and other bot functionality.

4. Data Sharing

AstraBot does not sell personal information. Information stored by AstraBot is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the bot or where required by law.

5. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored on infrastructure used to operate AstraBot. Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide AstraBot's features. Some information may remain until it is manually removed or the relevant server or bot data is reset.

7. Data Requests

If you have questions about information stored by AstraBot or would like to request deletion of information associated with your Discord account, contact Astra through the appropriate Discord server or account associated with the bot.

8. Children's Privacy

AstraBot is a Discord application and is subject to Discord's rules and requirements regarding age and use of its services.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be made available on this page.

10. Contact

For questions or concerns about AstraBot's privacy practices, contact Astra through the appropriate Discord server or account associated with the bot.
