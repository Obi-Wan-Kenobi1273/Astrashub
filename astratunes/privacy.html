🎧 AstraTunes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

AstraTunes is a Discord music bot developed as part of the AstraShub project. This Privacy Policy explains what information AstraTunes may process when you use the bot.

1. Information AstraTunes Processes

AstraTunes may process information provided through Discord when commands are used, including:

  • Your Discord user ID and username.
  • Your Discord server and channel information required to operate commands.
  • Voice channel information when AstraTunes joins or leaves a voice channel.
  • Music searches, song names, and URLs submitted to music commands.
  • Information necessary to process command requests and provide responses.
  • Technical error information when a command encounters an error.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by AstraTunes is used only for purposes related to operating, maintaining, securing, and improving the bot.

This may include:

  • Processing Discord commands.
  • Joining and leaving voice channels.
  • Playing requested music.
  • Managing music queues and playback.
  • Diagnosing technical errors.
  • Preventing abuse or misuse of the bot.
  • Maintaining the reliability and security of AstraTunes.

3. Music Services

AstraTunes may use third-party services or libraries to search for and retrieve information about requested music.

AstraTunes does not claim ownership of music, recordings, artwork, videos, or other content provided through third-party services. Users are responsible for using AstraTunes in accordance with applicable laws and the terms of the services they use.

4. Data Storage

AstraTunes is designed to process only the information necessary for its features. Information may be temporarily processed while a command, playback session, or voice connection is active.

AstraTunes does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment card information, or private messages.

5. Data Sharing

AstraTunes does not sell or rent personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary for AstraTunes to provide its functionality, such as Discord and services used to obtain music metadata.

6. Discord

AstraTunes operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own terms and privacy practices.

AstraTunes is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord Inc.

7. Data Requests

If you have questions about information associated with your use of AstraTunes, you can contact the AstraShub developer through the contact method provided on the AstraShub website.

8. Children's Privacy

AstraTunes is not specifically designed for children. Users should comply with Discord's applicable age requirements and policies when using Discord and AstraTunes.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AstraTunes gains new features, changes how information is processed, or when necessary to keep the policy accurate.

The latest version will always be available on this page.

10. Contact

For questions regarding AstraTunes or this Privacy Policy, please use the contact information provided through AstraShub.
